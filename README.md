# BladePulse

**BladePulse** brings your weapon to life with reactive audio feedback tied directly to your strikes.

Every swing feels sharper, every proc more impactful. Hear your combat.

---

## ⚔️ What It Does

BladePulse plays custom sound effects when specific combat events happen:

- **Extra Attacks / Procs** — Feel the power of bonus strikes
- **Parry** — Hear when your attacks are deflected
- **Glancing Blows** — Subtle feedback for weaker hits

Each event can trigger its own unique sound, making combat more immersive and responsive.

---

## 🎛️ Features

- Toggle each combat event on or off individually
- Global enable/disable switch
- Test buttons for every sound
- Clean, draggable settings window
- Minimap button for quick access
- Persistent settings (saved automatically)

---

## 🧭 How to Use

### Open Settings

- Type: `/bp`
- Or click the **minimap button**

### In the Settings Panel

- Enable or disable each event type
- Click **Test** to preview sounds instantly
- Use the global toggle to turn everything on/off

You can drag the window anywhere on your screen.

---

## 🔊 Custom Sounds

All sounds are located in:

```
Interface\AddOns\BladePulse\sounds\
```

Default files:

- `extra_attack.mp3`
- `parry.mp3`
- `glancing.mp3`

### To customize:

1. Replace any sound file with your own
2. Keep the **same filename**
3. Reload the game (`/reload`)

That’s it.

---

## 💡 Tips

- Use short, punchy sounds for best effect
- Lightsaber, metal clash, or sci-fi effects work great
- Lower volume files tend to feel more natural in combat
